Apply a Discount for Lack of Marketability to the equity conversion option only — not the debt component. Leave at 0% for publicly traded issuers. DLOM is a significant Level 3 input; ranges typically 10–35% for private companies depending on put option models or restricted stock studies.

HISTORICAL VOLATILITY LOOKUP — PUBLIC COMPANY DATA
Fetch actual price data from Yahoo Finance and compute annualised historical volatility. For public company options use the subject company. For private warrants, use a comparable public company as a volatility proxy — document peer selection in the sourcing log.

Enter a market-observed option price and back-solve for the implied volatility using Newton-Raphson iteration. Use to validate a volatility assumption or derive implied vol from a quoted price.

◈ Convertible Preferred Stock Valuation
Monte Carlo simulation (5,000 paths) — models liquidation preference, participation, conversion, price floor, default probability, and cumulative dividends. ASC 820 Level 3. Dual discount rates: risk-free for GBM drift, credit-adjusted for cash flow discounting.
